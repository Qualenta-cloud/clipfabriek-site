Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Clipfabriek is a private tool used only by its owner, the operator of the Clip Powerup channels, on his own computer. It has no other users.

What it accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube Data API): permission to upload videos to the owner's own channel and to read that channel's uploads, so the tool can confirm an upload arrived and never posts the same clip twice.
  • Instagram (Instagram API): permission to publish Reels and a first comment on the owner's own professional account, and to read that account's recent posts for the same check.

What it stores and where

The login tokens are stored encrypted in a database on the owner's computer. They are never sold, shared or sent anywhere except to Google and Meta when the tool calls their APIs. Videos stay on the owner's computer; for Instagram a video is placed for a few minutes behind a private, expiring link so Instagram can fetch it, and is then deleted.

The tool does not collect, store or process data about other people.

Google API Services

Clipfabriek's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) and in Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites. The owner can delete the stored tokens in the tool with "Ontkoppelen".
