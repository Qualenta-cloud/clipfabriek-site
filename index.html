Clipfabriek

Clipfabriek is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It turns long gaming streams into short vertical clips and, after the owner approves each clip by hand, uploads them to the owner's own channels: Clip Powerup on YouTube and Instagram.

It is not offered to other people and there is nothing to sign up for. It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload the owner's approved clips to his own channel and to check that an upload arrived.
